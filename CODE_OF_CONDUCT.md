# Code of Conduct

[中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration space. Treat people with dignity, critique ideas and evidence rather than individuals, and assume neither identity nor authority from an AI-generated contribution.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, publishing private information, deliberate disruption, and misrepresenting generated or unverified work as accepted human judgment.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may restrict participation when behavior threatens the community or project. Report conduct concerns privately to the security/contact channel published by the maintainers. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and routed to another maintainer when possible.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
